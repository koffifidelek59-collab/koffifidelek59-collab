### 🧑‍💻 About Me

I am an **Energy Systems Analyst** and **AI enthusiast**, currently completing a Master's in Energy and Green Hydrogen at **WASCAL, Abdou Moumouni University (Niger)**. My work focuses on applying machine learning and data science to solve energy challenges in Africa.

I hold a Master's in **Electronics, Electrical Engineering, Automation, and Computer Science (EEAI)** from **Félix Houphouët-Boigny University (Côte d'Ivoire)**.

I am passionate about the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence** and **Green Hydrogen**, and I aim to contribute to smart grid and clean energy projects across West Africa and beyond.

🔭 **I’m currently working on:** AI-based fault diagnosis, load forecasting, and smart grid flexibility  
🌱 **I’m currently learning:** Advanced Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Energy System Modelling  
👯 **I’m looking to collaborate on:** Research projects in renewable energy, green hydrogen, and AI for power systems  
💬 **Ask me about:** Python, Machine Learning, Energy Systems, Green Hydrogen  
📫 **How to reach me:** [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/koffi-fidele-kouame/) or [Email](mailto:koffifidelek59@gmail.com)
